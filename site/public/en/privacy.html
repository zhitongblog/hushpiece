Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Hushpiece (耳语同传) for Mac

In one sentence: Hushpiece collects, uploads and sells no data. Speech recognition, translation and speech synthesis use macOS's on-device models and happen on your Mac. Hushpiece has no servers, needs no account, and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

What we never do

  • Upload any audio, text or transcript to any server
  • Ask you to sign up, or collect your name, email, device identifiers or location
  • Use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Share data with third parties

Models provided by macOS

The first time you use a language, macOS downloads Apple's on-device speech recognition and translation models for it. The system does this under Apple's Privacy Policy; it does not go through Hushpiece and carries none of your meeting content.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to recognise what you say.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording: macOS files "hearing system audio" under this permission. Hushpiece uses audio only; it never looks at or saves your screen.

You can turn these off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Transcripts and deletion

Each meeting's text transcript is saved in the app's data folder (menu bar → Meeting records → Open folder). Delete the files, or the app, and they are gone. We have no access to them, so we cannot restore them either.

Recording notice

Hushpiece does not record audio, but it does turn the conversation into a text transcript. If your jurisdiction or company requires telling participants, please do so.

Children

Hushpiece is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: hushpiece@tobefree.app.
