An interpreter in your ear for every meeting

They speak English, Japanese, French… you read your own language, live. You speak yours, and the translation appears the moment you pause. Recognition, translation and speech all run on your Mac — the meeting never leaves it.

Free download · macOS

Three steps

A setup guide walks you through on first launch and checks each step for you.

  1. Pick languages, download modelsChoose what you speak and what they speak; macOS downloads the matching on-device models.
  2. Grant accessMicrophone (to hear you) and Screen & System Audio Recording (only to hear the other side of the meeting — the screen is never read, nothing is recorded).
  3. MeetClick the waveform icon in the menu bar → Start. The subtitle panel floats above your meeting and saves a transcript when you finish.
